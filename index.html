BizOps SOP Inter-Departmental Matrix

Golden Rule

All cross-departmental requests and project handoffs must be documented and coursed through official email correspondence & platforms to be queued or acted upon by BizOps or tracked on this board.

Priority SLAs

P1: Critical (4-8 Hrs) Client project halted, immediate revenue risk.
P2: Standard (24 Hrs) Routine contracts, billing triggers, site access.
P3: Low (3-5 Days) Case studies, non-urgent data requests.

Inter-Departmental Handoffs

  • Sales → BizOps: Signed Agreement, Client Contacts, Commercial Terms, Scope Brief.
  • BizOps → Tech: Technical Specs check, Resource assignment, GANTT Chart within 24h.
  • BizOps → Finance: Deposit invoice trigger, PO verification, Milestone billing clearance.
  • BizOps → Admin: Contracts/NDAs filing, Site Access Permits, Bonds (Surety/Performance/Retention), Accreditation.
