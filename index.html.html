Central Ops Board Architecture & SOP

BizOps Inter-Departmental Coordination Protocol

1 Golden Rule & Core Purpose

The Golden Rule: All requests/project handoffs must be documented and coursed through official email correspondence (bizops.mcps@gmail.com) or logged on the Central Ops Board for it to be queued or acted upon by BizOps.

2 Service Level Agreements (SLAs) & Priority Matrix

Priority Criteria Initial Response Resolution SLA
P1: Critical / Blocker Active client project halted, safety/legal issue, immediate revenue at risk. Within 1 Hour 4–8 Hours
P2: Standard Routine contract filing, invoicing triggers, logistics scheduling (>48h out). Within 4 Hours 24 Hours
P3: Low / Strategic Case study outreach, non-urgent data requests, future campaign alignment. Within 1 Business Day 3–5 Business Days

3 Standard Workflow Lifecycle

[1. Intake] → [2. Triage & Review] → [3. In Progress] → [4. Pending Sign-off] → [5. Completed / Closed]
